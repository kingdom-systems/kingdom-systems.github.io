Kingdom is a private collection of tools maintained by Matthew King. Its Google integration is configured as kingdom-google-api and supports authorised personal and work account workflows.

How Kingdom uses Google

The integration can organise selected email attachments, work with Google Drive files and use Google Calendar where the account’s permissions allow it. Each account grants its own permission through Google.

Gmail
Read messages, labels and attachments for selected workflows.
Drive
Read and manage files used in the owner’s workflows.
Calendar
Read and manage calendar information where configured.

Ordinary and AI-controlled workflows use separate Google OAuth clients and separate account tokens. Permissions and the intended task remain subject to the account owner’s approval.

Understand the data use

The privacy notice explains account access, storage, AI-assisted processing and how to withdraw access. This information site provides no account sign-in or Google data processing.
