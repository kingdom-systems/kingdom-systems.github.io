Privacy notice

Kingdom is maintained by Matthew King for private workflows using accounts authorised by their owners. This notice covers its ordinary and AI-controlled Google integrations.

Updated 13 October 2026

Data accessed and its purpose

Depending on the selected account and its consent, Kingdom may access Google account identity, Gmail messages, labels and attachments, Google Drive file content and metadata, and Google Calendar information. Gmail access is read-only. Drive and Calendar permissions can allow changes as well as reading.

This data is used to carry out the owner’s selected tasks, such as retrieving attachments, organising or updating files and working with calendar information. Permissions differ by account. Giving permission does not authorise every possible operation.

Storage and retention

OAuth client credentials and account refresh tokens are kept in 1Password and used by the private tools. Ordinary and AI-controlled access have separate credentials and account tokens.

Workflow data and derived outputs may be stored in local files or Google Drive, depending on the task. Any configured file-sync, backup or storage provider may hold copies. Retention depends on the workflow and the owner’s requirements; there is no single automatic deletion period for all outputs.

Sharing and AI-assisted processing

Workflows may upload or share selected content in Google Drive or another destination when the owner authorises that task. For AI-controlled work, selected Google content may be supplied to the AI service chosen by the owner to carry out the requested task. That provider’s retention settings also apply.

Kingdom’s use and transfer of Google API information must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data must not be sold or used for advertising. Google Workspace API data is excluded from developing, improving or training non-personalised AI or machine-learning models. The owner must confirm that any chosen processor and settings meet these requirements before providing Google user data.

Withdrawing access and deleting copies

You can withdraw Kingdom’s Google access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future use of the affected grant but does not remove files already created, shared or backed up.

For removal of stored credentials or retained outputs, contact the maintainer. Copies held in Google Drive, backups or another authorised service must also be addressed at their respective destinations.

This information website

These pages contain no sign-in, analytics scripts, cookies set by Kingdom or Google account data collection. The intended host, GitHub Pages, records visitor IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

Contact Matthew King at mking2709@gmail.com with access or privacy questions. Changes to data use must be disclosed before that use begins, with fresh consent where required.
